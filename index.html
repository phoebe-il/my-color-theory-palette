Color Theory Guide

My Color PaletteLight-to-Medium, Neutral-Warm

A palette built from color theory alone: your skin tone, your undertone, and the shade of your favorite lip color, used as the anchor for everything else.

I

Your Skin Tone

Skin tone has two parts: depth (how light or dark) and undertone (the hue underneath). Both decide which colors flatter you.

Light-to-Medium with a Neutral-Warm Undertone

Your depth sits in the light-to-medium band, and your undertone is neutral leaning warm: a balance of pink and golden-peach, with the gold slightly ahead. Your foundation shade, Beech, follows this exactly, a soft warm beige with a whisper of peach.

A neutral-warm undertone is flexible. It carries both soft pinks and warm peaches, as long as they are muted. Very cool, icy colors clash with the gold, and very orange or deep bronze colors overpower a light-to-medium depth.

II

Your Anchor Shade

Your goal palette is built from the lip color you already know suits you. Reading its properties tells us what the rest of the face should match.

Apple Toast, a warm rose-brown

4U2 Girlfriend's Mousse Lip Tint in No. 01 Apple Toast is a warm, toasty rose-brown: pink and brown blended into one muted shade. It works because it repeats what is already in your skin, warmth from the brown and softness from the rose.

III

The Goal: Nude with a Hint of Pink

The target is a palette close to your skin in value, muted in saturation, and warm in temperature, with rose as the single accent. Apple Toast sets the mood, and the shades below stay within its family.

Stay muted

Low saturation keeps colors close to skin. That is what reads as nude, not bare.

Keep the warmth

A slightly warm temperature matches the golden side of your undertone, so nothing looks ashy.

One pink accent

Let rose appear in a single place, the cheeks or lips, and repeat it softly elsewhere. This is the hint of pink.

IV

Cheeks

Cheeks carry the pink. Choose colors slightly lighter than Apple Toast so the lips stay the deepest point on the face.

Soft Rose
CHEEKS#E9A7A9

A clean, gentle pink with a little warmth. Light in value, low in saturation.

WHY IT WORKSPink on the apples of the cheeks imitates a natural blush. It repeats the rose in Apple Toast at a lighter value, so the two look related without matching.

Peachy Pink
CHEEKS#F2B5A7

A soft pink with peach mixed in, sitting between rose and coral.

WHY IT WORKSYour undertone has gold in it, so a little peach inside the pink keeps the cheeks from looking cold. It gives a warm, healthy flush that still reads pink.

Dusty Rose
CHEEKS#D98E8E

A muted rose with a touch of brown, greyed slightly to lower its intensity.

WHY IT WORKSIt is the closest cheek color to Apple Toast, because both share a brown-softened rose. It is the choice for evenings, when the whole face can be a little deeper.

Petal Glow
HIGHLIGHT#F5D2C4

A pale pinky-peach, one or two values lighter than your skin.

WHY IT WORKSHighlight works by being lighter than the skin around it. A pinky-peach tint does this without turning chalky, and it links the cheekbones to the pink blush below.

V

Lips

Lips are the deepest point of the palette. Apple Toast stays your anchor, with lighter and pinker neighbors for variety.

Apple Toast (your anchor)
LIPS#C97F78

Warm rose-brown, muted, mid-value.

WHY IT WORKSIts warmth matches your undertone and its muted saturation keeps the look nude. It defines the lips without competing with the cheeks.

Rose Nude
LIPS#D9A0A0

A beige-toned rose, lighter and pinker than Apple Toast.

WHY IT WORKSShifting one step toward pink and lighter gives a softer look. It sits close to the cheek pinks, so the face reads as one color story.

Peach Pink
LIPS#E5AB9C

A soft peachy pink, the lightest lip on this map.

WHY IT WORKSPeach undertones melt into a golden-leaning skin, so this is the freshest, most daytime option.

Nude Brown

A soft brown-nude with a little rose, a step deeper than Apple Toast.

WHY IT WORKSBrown-toned nudes share the warmth of a neutral-warm undertone. It is the most grown-up and low-key lip on the map.

VI

Eyes

Eyes stay in soft pinks, mauves and warm browns. Light shades open the eye, and the deepest shade is used sparingly at the outer corner.

Cream Lid
EYES#F3DCCB

A pale, warm cream just above skin value.

WHY IT WORKSA shade close to your skin evens out veins and shadows on the lid, so the eye looks rested. It is also the calm base for deeper shades.

Peach Nude
EYES#E9C4B0

A warm peach-beige with a touch of pink.

WHY IT WORKSIt echoes the peach in your undertone, so it adds gentle depth without looking dirty or tired.

Mauve Crease
EYES#C48E86

A muted mauve with a rosy-brown base.

WHY IT WORKSMauve behaves like a natural shadow. Its pink undertone stays flattering in the crease instead of turning grey, and it connects the eyes to the rose on your cheeks and lips.

Soft Brown
EYES#A8746B

A warm, rosy brown, never black or ashy.

WHY IT WORKSUse it only at the outer corner or lash line. It is the deepest eye color and stays in the same warm family as Apple Toast, so the eyes and lips balance.

Colors that fight your skin tone

Deep bronze, copper and dark chocolate sit far below your skin in value and far above it in saturation. On light-to-medium skin they look heavy and pull attention away from the soft pink. That is likely why your gifted palettes did not suit you. Icy cool pinks and blue-based mauves can also look ashy against the gold in your undertone.
